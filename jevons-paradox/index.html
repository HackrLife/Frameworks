A throughput model

Generation scales. Approval may not.

Connect production to a fixed review capacity. This is the same structure behind compute meeting grid capacity, or software findings meeting maintainer time.

06 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

A mechanism to test.
Not a law of endless demand.

What this page establishes

The arithmetic distinguishes adjusted growth from scaled growth. The sliders explore assumptions; they do not estimate real-world AI demand.

The marketing example comes from the supplied article and is illustrative. All slider results are computed, illustrative values.

The simple elasticity model assumes constant quality, fixed resource prices, full cost pass-through, unconstrained capacity, and constant elasticity.

Where the model can fail

Saturated demand: no useful backlog means real savings.

Capacity limits: volume can become queues and delays.

Uneven labour effects: more total output can coexist with fewer jobs in a particular role.

Evidence: the energy literature review cited below finds limited support for widespread scaled growth (what the literature calls backfire). AI examples here illustrate a possible mechanism; they do not establish it empirically.
